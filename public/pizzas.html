The headline

New York-style and Sicilian-style

Two pizza traditions, one kitchen — and a wood-fired oven that comes out once a week.

New York-style

The big, thin pie — cut in slices, meant for folding. Dinner tonight, no planning needed.

Sicilian-style

Thick, fluffy and baked in a tray, cut into squares — the style of Italy’s island south-west of Naples, not far from where the family is from.

Once a week, wood-fired

One night a week the kitchen fires the wood oven for a limited run of pies. Follow the shop’s Facebook page — those nights get announced there.

Slices, whole pies and lunch specials — the exact menu and prices land opening week.
