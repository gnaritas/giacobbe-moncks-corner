From Bacoli to Moncks Corner

A family story, two towns

Giacobbe Scotto grew up in Bacoli, less than 13 miles from Naples, in a house where his father made a whole year of tomato sauce in Italy before opening his own restaurant. Rachel — his wife and partner — grew up right here in Moncks Corner. When the two decided the time had come to open their own place, they chose her hometown: the small town they both fell in love with, and one of its most recognizable old buildings, at 108 W. Railroad Ave.

The goal, in Giacobbe’s own words, is true restaurant dining — brought to a town he believes is hungry for it.
